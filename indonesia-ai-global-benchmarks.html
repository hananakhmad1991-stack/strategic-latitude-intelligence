World Bank labour market signals

Indonesia's demand for advanced digital skills approximately tripled between 2021 and 2024. AI-related vacancy demand also approximately tripled over the same period, according to the World Bank report's narrative.

These are approximate growth statements: they are not presented as exact 200% measured increases, and should not be conflated with LinkedIn's separate 2025 hiring rate measure.
