How to read SLI data · Actual / Forecast / Announced / Estimated / Unverified
Actual / Observed

Measured or officially reported results. Always check the observation period and revisions.

Forecast / Projection

A future estimate conditional on a model, scenario, or publisher's assumptions—not a realized result.

Announced / Planned

Public commitment or project pipeline. Not proof of completed construction, disbursement, or operational capacity.

Estimated / Modeled

Calculated from stated assumptions. The model output may differ from official statistics.

Unverified / Media lead

A claim needing independent corroboration. It must not be treated as a verified event or investment fact.

N/A / Missing

Insufficient or unavailable evidence. Never interpret missing data as zero, no risk, or no investment.

Check source, geography, unit, publication date and coverage before comparing figures. Unified glossary & methodology ↗
