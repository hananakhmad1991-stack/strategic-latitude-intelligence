# Strategic Latitude Intelligence — Pre-Launch Quality Standard

**Scope:** Every published SLI dashboard, data visualization, regional/sector brief, source panel, chart, appendix and Research AI generative report.

## Sitewide quality gate
- Preserve publisher, original evidence link, observation/release date, measurement scope, units and time period for quantitative claims.
- Keep **observed**, **preliminary**, **estimated/modelled**, **forecast**, **announced**, and **unverified media lead** distinct. Project pipeline is not realized capital expenditure; operating capacity is not announced nameplate capacity.
- Align units, years, geographic boundaries and methodology whenever two statistics appear in one comparison, chart or recommendation.
- Reconcile duplicate assets, upstream vs downstream facilities, changed government officeholders, district/province labels and source vintages before publishing replacements.
- Licensing matters: public-domain citations do not automatically authorize redistribution or AI processing of proprietary tables. Never expose client reports in GitHub Pages or Owner Test.
- Automated watchers are **editorial leads**, not authorization to overwrite reviewed baseline numbers.
- Test all search/filter/export/map/chart functions for mobile and desktop. Ensure failures and unavailable data are explained, never silently substituted.
- Require clear glossary/abbreviation/appendix definitions, with cross-dashboard terms used consistently.
- CI structural auditing is mandatory; passing it does not imply a statistic is accurate or newly updated.

## Generative Research AI (priority)
- Filter evidence by sector, commodity, exact geography, date, and permitted rights before generation.
- Distinguish actually **cited in narrative**, **source for charts**, and **additional selected context** in the source panel.
- Build graph numbers from dated source-linked records, not invented AI numbers; omit incompatible visualizations.
- Present an analytical, evidence-based narrative with uncertainty, limitations and source dates. Finish with 3–5 **Kesimpulan dan Rekomendasi Strategis** pointers (English equivalent where appropriate).
- Limit Owner Test to a clearly labeled public, curated staging corpus. Do not imply live internet retrieval, independent verification or Pro/Enterprise readiness.
- Maintain rate limiting and per-request spending safeguards; avoid automatic paid retries without explicit cost approval.

## Outstanding release blockers
Full RAG/private corpus, publisher rights review, production subscriber login/entitlements, payment and pricing, user/session isolation, secure export, customer support, privacy and commercial policies, full regression/production test, and reliable public routing still need verification. The sitewide audit parses JSON and checks core structural provenance only; it is **not** a factual or licensing certification.
