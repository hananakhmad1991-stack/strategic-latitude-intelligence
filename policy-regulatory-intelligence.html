Separate Pasal 8 branches: Permen/Peraturan Lembaga, Pergub, Perbup/Perwali, and authorized technical regulations. A Pergub may expressly implement a provincial Perda; Perbup and Perwali act within their own regency/city authority and do not automatically derive from Pergub. Kepmen is often an administrative decision rather than a regulation. Inpres is an instruction to designated agencies, not a Perpres rung. WTO, ICSID, UNCITRAL, EU market regulations and U.S. BIS rules sit in separate international/foreign-market branches: their applicability is not determined by domestic legal rank. Priority policy-change alerts for SLI

Supersession and transition

PP 28/2025 vs PP 5/2021: new OSS licensing framework supersedes the older government regulation. PP 40/2025 vs PP 79/2014: the national energy policy changed, while older implementing instruments require transition review.

Mining-sector consolidated text must include UU 2/2025 and related implementing amendments; AI regulation announcements should not be treated as promulgated laws without publication.

Local-national harmonization

Jawa Barat RUED (Perda 2/2019), Jawa Tengah RUED (Perda 12/2018) and Kepulauan Riau RUED (Perda 4/2023) remain relevant planning references. Their consistency with the new PP 40/2025 should be evaluated, not assumed.

Do not infer that RUPTL planned substation additions amount to local available headroom, that energy storage MW is electricity consumed or that approved AI infrastructure financing equals realized capex.
