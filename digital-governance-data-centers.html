PDN-1: implementation status versus planned milestones

Critical investor interpretation: PDN-1 reported 100% building construction in March 2025, but certification, migration and operational readiness are separate matters. The report describes a migration roadmap spanning 2025–2028. BSSN's technical assessment is a readiness activity, not an absolute cybersecurity guarantee. Draft PDP implementing regulation and supervisory institution should not be presented as enacted solely on the basis of historical drafts. This page is a dated ministry-report snapshot, not live incident monitoring.
