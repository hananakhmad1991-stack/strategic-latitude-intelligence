AI compute data gaps

National operational GPU fleet, GPU-hour pricing, sustained AI training FLOPS, actual utilization, commissioned AI-specific IT load, and clean-power procurement are not quantified in the sources reviewed.

A national AI-readiness score should therefore not be constructed by summing generic data-center MW, project pipeline MW and digital training figures.

Sources: ASEAN Guide for Sustainable Data Centre Development (2025/2026 publication); CBRE Vietnam 2025; Komdigi ZanKore announcement (7 August 2026); SLI's earlier national estimate and Komdigi/Stanford data. All figures retain source links and historical date. No automatic changes are made to national capacity estimates.
