Methodological distinctions

Annual 2024 actual installed transformer MVA ≠ generation peak MW ≠ available firm capacity. The RUPTL 2025–2034 is a future plan; it must not be added to 2024 installed stock without actual completion records and deduplication.

Crosswalk is geographic/operational context only. It does not identify a specific feeder, GIS location or guaranteed service to any proposed data center.
