What the service covers

  • Define target expertise, industry, geography and conflict-of-interest requirements
  • Search and screen potential candidates from appropriate public and professional sources
  • Verify candidate identity, expertise, availability and willingness before presenting confirmed options
  • Coordinate introductions or consultations only after consent and client approval

How an engagement works

  1. Client sends a brief describing desired expertise and research purpose
  2. SLI reviews feasibility and sends a quotation or proposed sourcing mandate
  3. Candidates are identified and screened; no unverified candidate is represented as affiliated with SLI
  4. Client approves proposed expert, consultation terms, and fee before scheduling

What you receive

Possible outputs: screened shortlist, candidate profiles, arranged expert call, or interview synthesis—subject to the contracted scope.

Important boundaries

SLI does not currently advertise a standing expert panel. No expert is guaranteed to accept an engagement. Confidential or material non-public information must not be requested or shared.
