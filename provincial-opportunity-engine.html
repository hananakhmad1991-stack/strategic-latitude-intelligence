Interpretation & source boundaries

What the engine measures

A user-weighted relative combination of four observed 2025 provincial indicators. Percentile values are computed among provinces with available values, with ties handled equally. The mechanical stress equals −(household expenditure × shock + PMTB × shock) / nominal GRDP.

Distributional context

BPS Gini coefficients measure inequality of expenditure, not income, wealth or purchasing power. March 2026 Gini is used only to describe socioeconomic context alongside 2025 economic observations. It is never used to change the score or stress calculation.

What it deliberately does not measure

No investment return, solvency/default probability, land cost, permit readiness, grid headroom, clean-power availability, crime-based investment risk, political-actor ranking, or claim that project announcements are realized investment.

How to use it

Shortlist provinces, inspect observed bottlenecks and registered project milestones, then validate sector-specific infrastructure, legal requirements, company filings and energy readiness in the other SLI dashboards. Scores should not be used without examining underlying values.
