About SLI

SLI brings together public evidence, sector intelligence, data visualization and analytical tools. Publicly released data and dashboards are provided on an open-access basis, without subscriptions or fees.

Why SLI

We prioritize traceable sources, explicit methodology, clear limits and practical insights rather than unsupported claims.

Research by request

Commercial engagement is limited to individually scoped, by-request work such as custom research, commissioned sector reports, research collaboration and expert sourcing. Scope, fees and delivery terms are discussed and agreed in writing before work begins. Expert candidates are independent and are not automatically SLI affiliates.

Transparency

Research assessments are independent; referenced public institutions and data providers do not endorse SLI. Client information is handled confidentially.
