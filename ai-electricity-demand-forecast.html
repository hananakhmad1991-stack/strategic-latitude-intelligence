Risk & investment interpretation

Grid reinforcement financing

Data-center-driven substation, connection and transmission investment cannot credibly be monetized without location-specific peak demand, grid queue, transformer sizing, protection requirements, redundancy and PLN project budgets. This dashboard provides scenario MW demand, not invented rupiah investment totals.

Carbon intensity and procurement

Emission factor and zero-emission matched share are adjustable illustrative assumptions, not a verified 2026–2035 Indonesian grid emissions trajectory. Matching renewable electricity with demand by time and location would require hourly power and contractual evidence. Storage GW in RUPTL is not clean annual energy.
