Technology & regulation watchlist

Investor interpretation

Operational cloud availability ≠ planned AI-factory capacity

Microsoft and AWS have operating cloud regions; their corporate investment commitments include services and other spending, so they are not directly comparable to planned campus capex.

Electricity contract ≠ operational IT capacity

Batam's power agreements demonstrate development progress but do not prove cooling, computing equipment and commissioned service readiness.

Construction milestone ≠ utilization

Bekasi's construction-stage campuses require operational commissioning, power-on dates, and commercial occupancy validation.

Regulatory pipeline ≠ enacted policy

AI roadmap and AI ethics draft rules must be tracked against their latest legal status. PDP, cybersecurity, licensing and environmental obligations require separate diligence.
