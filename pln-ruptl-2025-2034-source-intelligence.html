Grid security & technical constraints

These figures are PLN planning criteria, not live measurements. A 30–38% generation reserve-margin planning range in Java–Madura–Bali does not establish unused capacity at a Bekasi or Batang substation. The 70–80% transformer-loading criterion is a planning trigger, not a real-time occupancy reading.
