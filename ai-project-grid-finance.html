Model boundaries

Modeling an unverified MW as a share of IT load is a user-controlled hypothetical scenario, never a disclosure or project forecast. Only projects with numerical MW display a scenario.

Annual GWh = assumed IT MW × PUE × utilization × 8.76. Energization schedule, build-out phases, power curtailment, supply redundancy and varying IT utilization are not modeled.

For investment underwriting, request contract capacity, transformer/substation delivery dates, PUE by load, energy procurement agreement, power price, connection milestones, GPU procurement and fibre diversity.

Historical evidence snapshot as of 16 October 2026. Reported financing facilities cannot be equated with disbursed capex. Source homepage links are starting points for further due diligence; they do not prove each field in the crosswalk. No facility location is assigned to unverified coordinates.
