## SLI Provincial Opportunity & Stress Engine (October 2026)

- **[Interactive 38-province opportunity engine](https://hananakhmad1991-stack.github.io/strategic-latitude-intelligence/provincial-opportunity-engine.html)** unites observed BPS 2021–2025 provincial expenditure GRDP, historical real growth, household expenditure intensity, fixed-capital formation (PMTB) intensity, and selected source-linked project milestones.
- **Source contracts:** `data/sli-bps-regional-intelligence.json`, `data/sli-project-registry.json`, and official inequality context `data/sli-bps-gini-2026.json`; no duplicate model-specific data snapshots or invented missing values. 2025 regional GRDP is preliminary; Gini is measured March 2026 and was published in August.
- **SLI-original lenses:** balanced regional activity, consumer-market, industrial expansion, and custom weights; transparent within-sample percentile composition, 0–100. The ranking is an adjustable screening fit, NOT expected investor returns, credit risk, project feasibility, or an official provincial grade.
- **Integrated growth × inequality intelligence:** cross-plot BPS 2025 provincial real-growth rates against independently measured March 2026 expenditure Gini for the same 38 provinces, with population-area switch (combined, urban, rural), national BPS area-specific benchmark, median provincial growth reference, interactive province selection, and analyst narrative explaining differences between each selected province, national Gini, and descriptive SLI geographic-region province Gini median/range. The observational periods are different and these relationships are not causal forecasts.
- **Strict separation:** Gini never enters the user-weighted opportunity-fit score or investment stress equation; income, wealth, purchasing power, distribution of growth and regional pooled-population Gini cannot be inferred from expenditure Gini. If Gini feed fails, economic screening continues and Gini is N/A. CSV export includes Gini values, population area, official national comparison and provenance.
- **Conditional accounting stress:** user-controlled percentage reductions in 2025 household spending and fixed-capital formation, with other components held unchanged. This is a partial expenditure-accounting counterfactual, not a model of real GDP, probabilities, taxes or propagation through supply chains.
- **Due diligence:** compare up to three provinces, inspect 2022–2025 real GDP growth, explore interactive growth-vs-PMTB scatterplot, see stage-specific project records and source URLs, generate a source-attributed SLI Analyst Brief for selected provinces, copy that brief for due diligence, and export the screening results to CSV.
- The project registry is curated, **not exhaustive**. Multi-site records cannot be summed across provinces; announcements are not actual expenditure. Political/civic statistics and criminality are *not* mixed into an unverified investment-risk rating. Grid headroom, permits, project economics and bankability remain unknown until independently checked.
- **Quality gate:** `.github/workflows/sli-provincial-opportunity-qa.yml` runs `tests/qa-sli-provincial-opportunity.mjs` when source data, analytical logic or interface changes.

## Whole-Dashboard Automation & Data Freshness (October 2026)

- **[SLI Overview](https://hananakhmad1991-stack.github.io/strategic-latitude-intelligence/#overview)**: shows live source date classifications and operational warning counters.
- **[SLI Data Freshness & Automation Control Center](https://hananakhmad1991-stack.github.io/strategic-latitude-intelligence/sli-data-automation-center.html)**: inventory of every `data/*.json`, GitHub Action writer, schedule, latest observation timestamp, API/QA failures and update method.
- **Source refresh:** `js/sli-live-data.js` intercepts same-origin public `data/*.json` GET requests and prefers the GitHub main-branch JSON over the GitHub Pages embedded copy. On failure, it falls back to Pages and exposes transport counters; it does not proxy third-party traffic or write new values. GitHub CDN/browser caching means this is **not a real-time guarantee**.
- **Scheduled audit:** `.github/workflows/sli-automation-health.yml` checks the whole source inventory four times per day, using source metadata and GitHub Actions run outcomes; malformed sources or failures are surfaced. It may open a consolidated issue for actual overdue/failed flows.
- **Publisher-edition detection:** `.github/workflows/sli-publication-watch.yml` checks 10 curated publisher listing pages daily and records new edition links as **review candidates**, never validated numeric source updates. Publishers using Javascript/blocked download are flagged as inaccessible/unparseable.
- **Actual automated numeric ingestion:** upstream structured services including World Bank annual WDI, source-attributed commodity/pricing and other scheduled structured pipelines. Each observation retains its own reference date, source and unit; scheduled execution does not imply a newer release existed.
- **News/discovery only:** legal changes, AI compute commissioning, personnel appointments, investment financing and other government/publisher announcements. A discovery lead cannot automatically overwrite a legal status, physical completion or validated leadership roster.
- **Curated/static reference datasets:** HEESI, RUPTL, PLN annual statistics, institutional maps, geocoded project locations, BPS/BI/Kemenkeu current press-release snapshot and source-derived analyst assessments. These **do not fully update themselves** until the relevant new edition/document is accessible and systematically extracted/validated. Published source vintages must not be silently overwritten by the next edition.

The objective is high and auditable automatic coverage, **not a claim that 100% of facts in the dashboard always update automatically**. Important exclusions, source failures and pending reviews are visible in the Control Center; date of monitoring is distinct from the underlying statistical observation period.

## Content standards
- Lawfully obtained, publicly attributable information.
- Check reuse rights, licenses and site terms for each source.
- Distinguish announcements, financing, construction, and operations.
- Include source URLs and reference dates when publishing data.
- Do not publish confidential client material or personal information without proper authorization.
- Do not give individualized investment or securities trading recommendations.
