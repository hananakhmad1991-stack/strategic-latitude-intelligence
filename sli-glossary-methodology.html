1 · How to read any SLI dashboard / Cara membaca

Observation ≠ publication ≠ refresh

Data measured in 2024 does not become 2026 output because the file was updated or rechecked in 2026. Show statistical period, issuer release, extraction or monitoring timestamp separately.

*Periode yang diukur ≠ tanggal publikasi ≠ waktu pembaruan dashboard.*

Plan ≠ contract ≠ operations

Project announcement, proposed MW, permits, MoU, signed PPA, project FID, construction, energization, commercial operation (COD) and actual investment describe different evidence states.

*Rencana, kontrak, konstruksi, operasi dan realisasi investasi harus dibedakan.*

Unknown ≠ measured zero

Missing/unknown values must remain empty, not zero. A stale page or inaccessible publisher cannot be treated as evidence of no change. Modelled projections require published assumptions and are not government outcomes.

*Data belum diketahui tidak boleh diisi nol atau estimasi tanpa label.*

4 · Data provenance & freshness / Asal dan kualitas data

Data-quality labels

  1. Official actual / realisasi resmi: source-linked observed data, dated, with reporting scope and revision policy.
  2. Official plan / rencana: approved RUPTL, development target or budget; not completed field output.
  3. Project announced / dilaporkan: sponsor claim, MoU, prospective financing or planned facility; identify issuer, status and verification.
  4. Analyst model / simulasi: conditional output of explicit inputs/assumptions; not official forecast or investment result.
  5. Monitoring lead / perlu ditinjau: newly discovered news, law or release candidate; no legal or numeric fact is changed until validated.
  6. Not verified / unavailable: missing accessible primary source, stale observation, unsupported count, or non-comparable geographic scope. Display unknown, not zero.

Minimum citation record

Include publisher, original URL, publication date, observation period, geography, reported unit, measurement definition, evidence status and extraction limitations. For cross-series comparison also reconcile numerator, denominator, frequency, current vs constant currency, ownership scope and revisions.

Update process: original structured feeds may refresh on schedule; scraped publisher headlines are candidates; annual PDF releases require deliberate extraction; institutional and legal changes must be checked against legal appointment or enactment documents. Open current source inventory ↗.

5 · Legal & institutional instruments / Hirarki regulasi

Pasal 7 UU 12/2011: UUD 1945 → TAP MPR → UU/Perppu → PP → Perpres → Perda Provinsi → Perda Kabupaten/Kota. Pasal 8 and implementing instruments include appropriately authorized Permen/agency rules, Pergub, Perbup and Perwali; do not assume they form one vertical chain. Kepmen/Kepres may be administrative decisions rather than generally binding regulation. Inpres is an instruction to designated executive recipients, not a new Pasal 7 rank.

International separation: ratification statutes/decisions and international obligations must be distinguished from domestic implementation. WTO dispute settlement is state-to-state; investor arbitration under ICSID/IIA requires consent; foreign award enforcement under the New York Convention has a different function. EU/US trade or technology rules may affect exports/counterparties without becoming Indonesian domestic legal hierarchy.

Legal monitoring headlines cannot, by themselves, change a rule's enacted/amended/repealed status. See source-linked legal map ↗ · See selected ex-ante, process, ex-post methodology ↗.

6 · Asset classification & geographic accuracy / Peta dan tahapan proyek

Industry versus facility

A WK/PSC is an upstream concession; oil refinery processes crude; LNG facility liquefies gas; metal smelter processes mineral feed; coal mine produces coal; PLTU consumes it as fuel. One family, multiple different asset types.

Status and ownership

Record operator, licensee, owner, parent and investor roles separately. Proposed capacity, commissioned unit, construction, signed contract and historical asset status must not be combined as additive asset count without de-duplication.

Pin & source geography

A province-only proxy must never be shown as a surveyed facility coordinate. Site, concession boundaries, operational unit and corporate office are distinct geographic objects. Complete original map classification & evidence legend ↗.
