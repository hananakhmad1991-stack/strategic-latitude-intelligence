Investment interpretation

Growth and macro stability are context for capital costs, import dependencies and consumer demand; they do not establish causation or validate individual project revenues. Trade in merchandise (BPS) differs from WDI goods and services; BOP differs from current account; national BPS poverty is not the World Bank PPP poverty rate. WDI unemployment estimates may not match Sakernas, and WDI Gini 0–100 differs from BPS 0–1.

Government debt-to-GDP is intentionally marked unavailable until a recent official figure with a clearly defined government coverage and denominator is verified. Data not reported ≠ zero.
