Data methodology, sources & limitations

Data states: observed, forecast, announced, modeled, unverified, and N/A. Data freshness and publisher attribution are required before using a number. These modules use a curated ticker list; issuer businesses and IDX symbols can change. Green/red sector narratives are conditional hypotheses, not forecast price returns.

Primary reference sources: IDX issuer directory, BI JISDOR, IDX financial disclosures, and SLI glossary.

Potential signals such as geopolitical shocks, foreign flow or commodity windfalls are not causal proof of corporate earnings.
