Foreign direct investment · Entry screening

For factories, energy projects and digital infrastructure, daily share-price moves are not a substitute for operational due diligence. No automatic Go/No-Go grade without project-specific evidence.

FDI evidence checklist

Regulatory & ownership

Permits, business classification, foreign ownership rules, land title, environmental approval and applicable incentives.

Infrastructure & supply

Power delivery, energy cost, reliability, water, road/port connectivity, skilled labor and contracted inputs.

Geopolitical & trade

Origin rules, tariffs, sanctions exposure, equipment dependencies, offtake markets and cross-border payment channels.

Project economics

Financing, FX sensitivity, cost of capital, taxes, capex evidence and scenario downside.

Data methodology, sources & limitations

Data states: observed, forecast, announced, modeled, unverified, and N/A. Data freshness and publisher attribution are required before using a number. These modules use a curated ticker list; issuer businesses and IDX symbols can change. Green/red sector narratives are conditional hypotheses, not forecast price returns.

Primary reference sources: IDX issuer directory, BI JISDOR, IDX financial disclosures, and SLI glossary.

Potential signals such as geopolitical shocks, foreign flow or commodity windfalls are not causal proof of corporate earnings.
