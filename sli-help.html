Help & FAQ

Find answers about the intelligence platform, research services, expert sourcing and account support.

What is Strategic Latitude Intelligence?

SLI provides Indonesia-focused intelligence, research tools, sector monitoring and expert sourcing services.

Can I request a custom report?

Yes. Describe the topic, intended use, scope and timeline through our official email. Work is subject to a separate agreement.

Are all Expert Connect candidates part of SLI?

No. Publicly discovered profiles are candidates only. Credentials, availability and willingness to participate are verified before any introduction or engagement.

How do I get help with my account?

Email contact@strategiclatitude.co.id. Never send passwords, OTP codes or secret API keys.

Can I reuse data and charts?

Rights depend on the underlying dataset, license and subscription. Verify source conditions and citations before redistribution.
