07 · Investment impact

Input-output multiplier readiness & conditional scenario

The robust approach is a sector-by-region input-output model using a compatible transactions matrix and the Leontief inverse. SLI has BPS national Input-Output 2020 publication references, but not yet a verified province × industry multiplier coefficient for the selected opportunity. Accordingly, no arbitrary multiplier or job creation figure is presented.

Assumed spending / 2025 GRDP—Size comparison only, NOT GRDP impact
Conditional gross output—IDR billion · requires user-supplied sourced coefficient
Estimated employment / regional value addedUnavailableRequires suitable IO labor and value-added coefficients
No multiplier results will appear until a coefficient and its source are supplied. The system does not verify user-entered multipliers automatically.

Framework: gross output response Δx=(I−A)⁻¹Δf. Entering a coefficient does not establish its validity for the selected province, period or project. Value-added multipliers, displacement, imported inputs, leakages and jobs require separate verified evidence; Δgross output must never be labeled an increase in GRDP.
