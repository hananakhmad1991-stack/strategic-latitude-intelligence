Investor due-diligence rules

A system peak load is NOT spare electricity; transmission circuit-km is NOT available MW; substation installed MVA is NOT firm N-1 headroom. Project-level power supply must be matched to a verified local network node, load queue, firm available MW and energization timing before asserting a grid stress conclusion.

Historic realized indicators are from PLN Statistics, while planned additions are from the PLN RUPTL 2025–2034. Each must carry its own effective period and measurement unit.
