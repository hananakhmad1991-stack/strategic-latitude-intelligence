Methodology, formulas & definitions

Data hierarchy: all scenario inputs are user-editable illustrative assumptions unless individually cited with a source, value date and unit. The published data center capacity context has unresolved IT-versus-facility classification and is not silently used as modeled IT capacity.

AI power: annual facility kWh = IT MW × 1,000 × utilization × PUE × 8,760. Operating surplus = annual service revenue − electricity bill − other modeled operating costs. Break-even tariff divides revenue after other costs by energized kWh. Rationing is modeled as a proportional fall in revenue and delivered kWh; real-world backup generation, contracts and outage consequences are omitted.

Commodity exposure: surplus = revenue after assumed demand loss + assumed customer cost pass-through − shocked commodity costs − shocked logistics costs − other modeled costs. Buffer runway = available inventory days / 7. Uncovered supply gap = max(0, delayed days − inventory days). No automatic relationship between pass-through and customer demand is inferred.

Fuel import: import bill in IDR = barrels × oil USD/bbl × IDR/USD. Apply the oil-price and exchange-rate stress together, once. The cost-neutral oil price under shocked FX is base price × base FX / stressed FX. This is not a budget subsidy, bankruptcy, capital control or sovereign risk forecast.

Breaking point caveat: model thresholds depend on the specified revenue/cost scope and simplifying assumptions. They are not externally verified investment ratings, default probabilities or advice to exit.
