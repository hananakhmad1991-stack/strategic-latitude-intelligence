Methodology, data gaps and independent verification

Risk factor inputs are manually supplied 0–100 proxies. The media signal monitor is keyword triage, not independently audited NLP. No validated country sentiment/GPR history, social platform ingestion, officially calibrated probabilities or portfolio-level factor covariance is connected.

Scenario percentile loss uses exactly three user-assumed outcomes and probabilities. Do not cite it as statistical VaR; authentic investment VaR requires defensible distributions, calibration and backtesting. News does not automatically change macro/meso/micro risk scores.

World outlines: Natural Earth 1:110m, via public-domain GeoJSON. Investments: SLI investment register. News: SLI research signal radar. Keyword-watch alerts compare media headline counts against user-set thresholds; they are not confirmed event trends. No automatic trade execution, financial advice or push notifications are enabled.

Independent SLI geopolitical research · Quantitative scenario exploration, not investment, legal or trading advice. Published source dates and coverage limitations must be read before making decisions.
