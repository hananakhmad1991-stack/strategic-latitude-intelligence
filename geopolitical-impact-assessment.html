Sources, definitions & data limitations

Primary source: Lowy Institute, Asia Power Index, Indonesia country profile, 2025 edition. Open original Indonesia profile ↗.

Different measures: Comprehensive power is Lowy’s weighted index, not a simple sum of eight displayed scores. Bilateral trade shares reflect 2024 data with a different denominator in each influence view. Rank changes are positions; score changes are index points.

What SLI does and does not do: this independently designed page presents a dated Lowy snapshot. It does not invent an SLI geopolitical risk score, calculate portfolio losses, recreate Lowy’s proprietary methodology, represent current-year live data, or provide trading recommendations.
